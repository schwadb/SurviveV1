Text → Morse
SOS is ··· ——— ···. Three of anything (whistles, flashes, fires) is a distress signal.
Morse → Text
Use . and - (or · and —), a space between letters, and / or three spaces between words.
Practice key
Short press = dot, long press = dash (timing follows the WPM slider). Pause to end a letter, pause longer for a space.
Reference
