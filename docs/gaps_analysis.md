# SurviveV1 Gap Analysis
## Based on Top Survivalist Expert Recommendations

Compiled from research on: Creek Stewart, Cody Lundin, Les Stroud, Dave Canterbury,
John "Lofty" Wiseman (SAS), James Wesley Rawles, Dr. Joseph Alton (Doom and Bloom),
Fernando "Ferfal" Aguirre, Laurence Gonzales, Sandor Katz, and others.

---

## 🔴 TIER 1 — CRITICAL GAPS (Absent from v1, life-or-death importance)

### 1. Advanced Survival Medicine
**What's missing:** The current repo has basic first aid. It lacks:
- **"The Survival Medicine Handbook"** — Dr. Joseph & Amy Alton (700+ pages, covers suturing, childbirth, dental, antibiotics, surgical procedures). THE definitive text.
- **"Emergency War Surgery"** — NATO/Borden Institute (FREE, public domain) — damage control surgery, trauma management
- **"Special Operations Forces Medical Handbook"** — US SOCOM (FREE PDF) — the medic's bible
- **"Armageddon Medicine"** — Dr. Cynthia Koelker — antibiotic stockpiling, drug equivalences
- **"Ditch Medicine"** — Hugh Coffee — trauma in remote settings
- Antibiotic dosing reference for collapse scenarios
- Drug equivalence charts (veterinary ↔ human medications)

### 2. Emergency Childbirth / Obstetrics
**What's missing entirely:** One of the highest-stakes grid-down scenarios.
- **"Emergency Childbirth"** — Gregory White (public domain, FREE PDF)
- **"Ina May's Guide to Childbirth"** — Ina May Gaskin
- **"Spiritual Midwifery"** — Ina May Gaskin
- **"Heart & Hands"** — Elizabeth Davis
- **"A Book for Midwives"** — Hesperian (FREE at hesperian.org)
- Normal delivery protocols, complications, postpartum hemorrhage

### 3. Dental Emergency Skills
**What's missing:** Zero dental content in v1.
- **"Where There Is No Dentist"** — Murray Dickson (Hesperian, FREE)
- Tooth extraction technique, dental blocks, abscess I&D
- Temporary filling materials (zinc oxide eugenol, Cavit)
- Oil of cloves/eugenol for pain management

### 4. Psychiatric & Mental Health in Collapse
**What's missing entirely:** Arguably the most neglected survival topic.
- **"Where There Is No Psychiatrist"** — Vikram Patel (Hesperian, FREE)
- **"Psychological First Aid"** — WHO/NCTSN (FREE PDF)
- **"Deep Survival"** — Laurence Gonzales — why some live and others die (psychology)
- **"The Unthinkable"** — Amanda Ripley — disaster psychology
- Medication management for existing psychiatric patients
- Grief processing, PTSD prevention, group trauma response
- Decision fatigue in extended emergencies
- **The psychological cost of violence** — almost never addressed in prepper content

### 5. Community Organization & Social Survival
**What's missing:** Individual/family focus only. Community = survival multiplier.
- **"A Paradise Built in Hell"** — Rebecca Solnit — how communities ACTUALLY behave post-disaster (counters the "everyone turns savage" myth with historical evidence)
- **"The Knowledge"** — Lewis Dartnell — rebuilding civilization from scratch
- **"The Modern Survival Manual"** — Fernando "Ferfal" Aguirre (Argentina 2001 real collapse experience)
- Community governance and conflict resolution
- Resource pooling vs. hoarding dynamics
- Skilled roles inventory for a community

### 6. Sanitation Without Infrastructure
**What's missing:** Sewage failure causes disease outbreaks within 72 hours.
- **"The Humanure Handbook"** — Joseph Jenkins (**FREE at humanurehandbook.com** — full book)
- Composting toilet construction
- Human waste pathogens and safe composting temperatures
- Urine diversion systems

### 7. Animal Husbandry
**What's missing:** Zero livestock content in v1. Long-term food = animals.
- **Storey's Guide series** — Raising Goats, Chickens, Pigs, Dairy Goats, Rabbits (Storey Publishing — the standard)
- **"The Independent Farmstead"** — Shawn & Beth Dougherty
- **"Keeping a Family Cow"** — Joann Grohman
- Disease management (Newcastle, mastitis, bloat)
- Breeding cycles and genetics
- Butchering and processing

## 🟠 TIER 2 — SIGNIFICANT GAPS (Present but shallow, or entirely absent)

### 8. Seed Saving (not just buying seeds)
**What's missing:** Most repos mention buying seeds, not saving them.
- **"Seed to Seed"** — Suzanne Ashworth (THE definitive seed saving reference — isolation distances, fermentation for tomatoes, biennial crops)
- Seed bank construction and long-term storage (mylar, oxygen absorbers, silica gel)
- Open-pollinated vs. hybrid distinction
- Minimum population sizes to avoid inbreeding depression
- Seed viability testing

### 9. Beekeeping
**What's missing:** Not in v1 at all. Critical for pollination and medicine.
- **"The Beekeeper's Handbook"** — Diana Sammataro
- **"Natural Beekeeping"** — Ross Conrad
- **"Top-Bar Beekeeping"** — Les Crowder (low-cost equipment approach)
- Hive disease management (varroa mites — colony collapse)
- Honey as wound treatment (Manuka properties, hygroscopic action)
- Beeswax applications: candles, waterproofing, leather care
- Mead brewing from honey

### 10. Blacksmithing
**What's missing:** Mentioned but no actual technical content.
- **"The Complete Modern Blacksmith"** — Alexander Weygers
- **"Practical Blacksmithing"** — M.T. Richardson (4 volumes, public domain)
- **"The Backyard Blacksmith"** — Lorelei Sims
- Improvised forge construction (brake drum + hair dryer)
- Basic tool repair: hammer eyes, knife making, hook and hinge making
- Coal vs. charcoal vs. propane forge options

### 11. Leather Brain Tanning
**What's missing:** Clothing and gear longevity without stores.
- **"Deerskins into Buckskins"** — Matt Richards (brain tanning — the definitive guide)
- Brain tanning: preparation, smoking, finishing
- Bark/vegetable tanning with natural tannins
- Hide preparation: fleshing, dehairing, membrane removal
- Rawhide vs. buckskin properties

### 12. Soap Making from Wood Ash
**What's missing:** Sanitation and hygiene without supply chains.
- **"The Soapmaker's Companion"** — Susan Miller Cavitch
- Lye extraction from wood ash (leaching)
- Saponification ratios for animal fats vs. vegetable oils
- Cold process vs. hot process
- Soft soap (potassium hydroxide) vs. hard soap (sodium hydroxide)

### 13. Fermentation & Food Preservation (deeper content)
**What's missing:** Current v1 has basic brewing; missing the breadth.
- **"The Art of Fermentation"** — Sandor Katz (the definitive text — covers everything)
- **"Wild Fermentation"** — Sandor Katz
- Lacto-fermentation (sauerkraut, kimchi, pickles) — preserves without canning equipment
- Vinegar making (acetic acid for cleaning, preservation, cooking)
- Kvass, kefir, yogurt, kombucha
- Medicinal tincture making with ethanol
- Alcohol as fuel and barter item

### 14. Cheese Making & Dairy Processing
**What's missing:** Calorie-dense preservation, requires livestock knowledge.
- **"Home Cheese Making"** — Ricki Carroll (the standard)
- **"The Art of Natural Cheesemaking"** — David Asher
- Natural rennet sources (fig leaves, nettles, thistle flowers) — no commercial supply needed
- Whey utilization
- Cave/root cellar aging

### 15. Water Well Construction
**What's missing:** Storage only in v1; sourcing without mains is different.
- **"Hand-Dug Wells and Their Construction"** — S.B. Watt & W.E. Wood (IT Publications, FREE PDF)
- **"Wells Construction: Hand Dug and Hand Drilled"** — Peace Corps (FREE, public domain)
- Site selection and water table reading
- Concrete ring casing vs. stone-lined
- Contamination prevention (sanitary seal)
- Hand pump installation

### 16. Greywater System Design
**What's missing:** Water reuse essential for long-term homesteading.
- **"Create an Oasis with Greywater"** — Art Ludwig (definitive guide)
- Three-bucket vs. constructed wetland systems
- Mulch basins
- Plants safe for greywater irrigation

### 17. Root Cellar Construction
**What's missing:** Food storage without refrigeration.
- **"Root Cellaring"** — Mike & Nancy Bubel (Storey Publishing)
- Site selection (north-facing slope)
- Temperature and humidity zones by crop
- Ventilation design
- Pest exclusion

### 18. Candle Making & Primitive Lighting
**What's missing:** Power is not permanent.
- Tallow dip candles from rendered fat
- Rushlight construction (simplest primitive lamp)
- Oil lamp design
- Beeswax vs. tallow vs. bayberry comparison

### 19. Barter Economy & Real Collapse Economics
**What's missing:** Most valuable skills and goods are never documented.
- **"The Modern Survival Manual"** — Fernando Aguirre (Argentina 2001 — REAL experience)
- **"When Money Dies"** — Adam Fergusson (Weimar hyperinflation — barter in practice)
- Most valuable barter skills historically: medical, alcohol, food, mechanical
- Avoiding exploitation and price gouging as recipient
- Accounting for barter systems in community

### 20. Security & Perimeter Defense
**What's missing:** Mentioned but no tactical doctrine.
- **"Contact!"** — Max Velocity (post-collapse tactical)
- **"Holding Your Ground"** — Joe Nobody
- **"The Reluctant Partisan"** Vol. I & II — John Mosby
- Layered defense concepts (delay, detect, deny)
- Noise/light/litter discipline
- Community mutual defense vs. individual fortress
- Non-lethal deterrents
- Dogs and livestock as early warning systems

## 🟡 TIER 3 — SHALLOW COVERAGE (In v1 but needs depth)

### 21. Morse Code / CW
**Current v1:** Mentions ham radio but not CW.
- **"The Art and Skill of Radio-Telegraphy"** — W. Pierpont N0HFF (FREE PDF)
- LCWO.net — Learn CW Online (browser-based, free)
- G4FON Koch Trainer (free software)
- CW works at signal levels where voice fails; can run on milliwatts
- SOS (... --- ...) universally recognized, no radio required (mirror, flashlight)

### 22. Radio Programming (CHIRP)
**Current v1:** Mentions Baofeng but not programming.
- CHIRP software — free, open-source, standard for Baofeng programming
- RepeaterBook.com — find local repeaters
- Emergency frequencies to pre-program: MURS, GMRS, 146.520 MHz (2m calling), NOAA
- Frequency coordination for community nets

### 23. Military Hand Signals
**Current v1:** Not present.
- **FM 21-60 Visual Signals** — US Army (FREE, public domain)
- Core signals: HALT, FREEZE, ENEMY CONTACT, COLUMN, LINE, FIRE, CEASE FIRE
- Ranger Handbook (SH 21-76) — FREE PDF

### 24. Pressure Canning Safety (detailed)
**Current v1:** Scripts mention canning but no safety protocol detail.
- NCHFP (nchfp.uga.edu) — authoritative USDA-funded tested recipes
- Botulism mechanism and why only pressure canning (240°F) kills spores in low-acid foods
- Altitude adjustment tables
- Common failure modes and detection

### 25. Solar Food Drying
**Current v1:** Not present.
- Peace Corps/VITA solar dryer plans (FREE)
- FAO technical guides on drying
- Box dryer vs. indirect solar dryer construction
- Temperature requirements for pathogen kill vs. nutrient preservation

### 26. Permaculture Design
**Current v1:** Brief mentions in homesteading content.
- **"Gaia's Garden"** — Toby Hemenway (best accessible permaculture book)
- **"Permaculture: A Designers' Manual"** — Bill Mollison (the technical bible)
- Zone design (0-5) for energy efficiency
- Guild planting (companion plants around fruit trees)
- Food forest establishment
- Water harvesting earthworks (swales, berms)

## 🧠 EXPERT RECOMMENDATIONS SUMMARY

### Creek Stewart (Weather the Storm, Fat Guys in the Woods)
- Emphasizes the "Will to Survive" as the #1 skill
- Prioritizes: shelter, water, fire, food IN THAT ORDER
- Stresses improvisation over gear dependency

### Dave Canterbury (Pathfinder School)
- 10 Cs of Survivability framework
- Heavy emphasis on primitive skills over modern gear
- Cutting tools, combustion, cover, containers, cordage, cotton, cargo tape, compass, candling, canvas

### Cody Lundin (Aboriginal Living Skills School)
- Thermoregulation as #1 priority
- Primitive living skills (brain tanning, earth ovens, hide shelters)
- Emphasizes calm mindset over equipment

### Les Stroud (Survivorman)
- Real-world emphasis on psychological preparedness
- Signaling and rescue as priorities when lost
- Urban survival often more dangerous than wilderness

### John "Lofty" Wiseman (SAS Survival Handbook)
- PLAN mnemonic: Protection, Location, Acquisition, Navigation
- Comprehensive global survival scenarios
- Military-derived techniques adapted for civilian use

### James Wesley Rawles (SurvivalBlog)
- "Beans, Bullets, Band-Aids" framework
- Community retreat model vs. lone wolf
- Legal/financial preparedness alongside physical
- Amateur radio as essential communications

### Fernando "Ferfal" Aguirre (The Modern Survival Manual)
- Argentina 2001 collapse — REAL experience, not theoretical
- Key insight: **Cities did NOT empty** — rural isolation was dangerous, not urban
- Economic collapse ≠ zombie apocalypse — keep skills marketable
- Importance of gold/silver as store of value
- Security was the #1 daily concern, not food or water
